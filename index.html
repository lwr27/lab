Player Development

Coaching &
Improvement

Improvement is at the heart of everything here. Table tennis rewards consistency, focus and smart practice — and with the right support, players of all ages develop quickly and confidently.

  • Technical coaching for stroke play and tactics
  • Match-play development and game understanding
  • Junior progression pathways into competition
  • Support for established league and competitive players

The Mission
"Growing table tennis across the South West — one player,
one club, one community at a time."

Table tennis builds confidence, sharpens concentration, and creates community. Through clubs, coaching and competition, the aim is to make the sport accessible to everyone across Bath, Bristol, Keynsham and the wider South West.
